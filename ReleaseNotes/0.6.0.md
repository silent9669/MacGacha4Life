# MacGacha4Life 0.6.0

- Home is now a dashboard. It lists what needs your attention first, then your games with a bar showing how much space each one takes, the newest events and your playtime.
- Playtime statistics stay on your Mac. See this week with a trend arrow, your streak as a heatmap or a month calendar, and your longest and average session, peak hour and launches.
- A game's page fits in one window. Every launch setting sits on the page with no settings window, and the game art keeps its full size. Playtime shows by week or month, with a calendar of the days you played.
- The sidebar is a compact Liquid Glass rail. The app icon is Home, followed by your games, Dependencies, Help & Performance, Diagnostics and Settings.
- Dependencies has a new look. Set Up All installs what every game needs, each dependency lists its versions with pin, remove and show in Finder, and Clean Up removes old versions that nothing uses.
- Nine optional color themes: four Catppuccin flavors, Tokyo Night, Dracula, Nord, Gruvbox Dark and Rosé Pine. The default look stays native. Pick one in Settings → General.
- Settings → Games can export your launch settings as a backup, import them again, and export your playtime as a CSV file.
- Settings → General has a Reduce Motion switch that stops the launcher's animations.
- The menu bar Quick Play shows today's playtime.
