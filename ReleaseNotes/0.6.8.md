# MacGacha4Life 0.6.8

- MG4L now tells macOS it supports game controllers, so your controller drives MG4L itself, in the style of Apple's Games app. As soon as a controller connects, the focused control lifts with a soft glow (Play first when the page has it), and a legend at the bottom shows your controller's own buttons: L1/R1 switch pages, Cross/A selects and Circle/B goes Home. The D-pad or left stick moves between controls.
