# MacGacha4Life 0.4.0

A full redesign of the launcher window.

- Dependencies, Help & Performance and Diagnostics are now pages in the main window, not sheets. Pick them in the sidebar.
- A game's backend, frame limit and Game Mode sit in the window toolbar. Press them to open the game's settings.
- The Play bar is built from separate Liquid Glass pieces, with the system's prominent button for the main action.
- The window has a standard title bar and toolbar again.
- New social preview and refreshed screenshots.
