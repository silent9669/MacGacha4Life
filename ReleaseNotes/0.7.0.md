# MacGacha4Life 0.7.0

- Controller use is smoother: the highlight moves at once and lifts the control with a soft glow instead of a box, and the legend at the bottom is larger and easier to read, with Move, Pages, Select and Home in your controller's own buttons.
- A game's page shows playtime as a bar chart, this week by default or this month. The calendar moved to Home, where each day's colour shows how long you played.
- Settings on a game's page use switches instead of checkboxes.
