# MacGacha4Life 0.6.6

- A DualSense on the PlayStation setting now vibrates over Bluetooth and USB, and games keep their PlayStation buttons. While a game runs, MG4L follows the game's own sound (only that game, nothing is recorded or stored) and turns hits, explosions and other deep sounds into vibration. The first time, macOS asks to let MG4L capture the game's audio.
